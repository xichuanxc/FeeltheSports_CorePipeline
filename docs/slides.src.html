Feeling the Tennis Game

Acoustic racket-strike detection for synchronised haptic feedback

Chuan Xi Supervised by: Assoc. Prof. David Nichols & Dr. Jemma König University of Waikato COMPX520 · 2026
The question

On a screen you can only see and hear

  • In person, you feel the physical energy of the game.
  • Hard hits and fast rallies most of all.
  • Through a screen, that channel is gone.
  • Giving it back is what Feel the Sports is for.
Research question Can we detect every racket strike in tennis audio, and drive a phone to vibrate in time with it?

And does that raise a viewer’s sense of immersion?

Find them, then name them

Conventional · where

Spectral-flux peak detection

  • Band-pass, then pick the sudden rises.
  • Finds 98% of events.
  • Says nothing about what any of them is.
Learned · what

Log-mel slice into a small CNN

  • 150 ms around each candidate, five classes.
  • 0.84 accuracy on a match never trained on.
  • Trained on exactly what peak detection surfaced.
Why both Peak detection is what makes a dataset exist at all. The model then hands the labelling back a suggestion that is right five times in six, so a person confirms instead of deciding.
What was built

The VideoPlayer program

Plays the match, drives the phone
  • Reads a timeline of events for the video.
  • Every event carries its loudness, scaled within that video, which becomes the strength of the buzz.

The answer

How the study ran

  • Two clips: one with vibration, one without.
  • Order balanced across participants.
  • Sound on in both, as anyone would actually watch.
  • A questionnaire before, and a longer one after.
  • On a Samsung Galaxy A03, a budget phone, not a flagship.
  • Ethics approved; participants identified only by code.

What twelve people said

4.67 of 5 · noticed, fits, in time nobody doubted the buzzes land in the right place
3.36 of 5 · enjoyed, engaging, would install six would install it, five would not
The answer that explains the split

“When I’m not focused it helps me focus. When I’m focused it distracts me from watching.”

Still open
  • Buzz strength is relative to each video.
  • Highlights, not live — and not yet running live either.
  • A bigger study, with more people.
Where the rest of this project goes The detector works. Finding who the feeling suits is the open half.
